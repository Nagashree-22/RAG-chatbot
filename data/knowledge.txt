


========================================
EMOTION-AWARE HEALTH KNOWLEDGE BASE
========================================

JOY

Joy is a positive emotion that makes people feel happy, excited, and satisfied. Sharing joyful moments with family and friends can strengthen relationships and improve overall well-being.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Celebrate your achievements.
- Spend time with loved ones.
- Continue healthy habits.
- Practice gratitude.
- Capture happy memories.

------------------------------------------------------------

SADNESS

Sadness is a natural emotion that everyone experiences at times. It can occur after disappointment, loss, stress, or loneliness. It usually improves with support, self-care, and time.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Listen to calming music.
- Go for a short walk.
- Talk to someone you trust.
- Maintain a healthy sleep schedule.
- Do activities you enjoy.

------------------------------------------------------------

ANGER

Anger is a normal emotion, but uncontrolled anger can affect relationships and health.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Take deep breaths.
- Count slowly before responding.
- Walk away from stressful situations.
- Exercise regularly.
- Express your feelings calmly.

------------------------------------------------------------

FEAR

Fear is the body's response to danger or uncertainty. It can be reduced by relaxation and positive thinking.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Practice deep breathing.
- Stay calm.
- Focus on one step at a time.
- Talk with trusted people.
- Avoid negative overthinking.

------------------------------------------------------------

LOVE

Love helps improve emotional well-being and creates stronger relationships.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Spend time with loved ones.
- Show appreciation.
- Be kind.
- Respect others.
- Express gratitude.

------------------------------------------------------------

STRESS

Stress can happen because of studies, work, relationships, or financial problems. Long-term stress may affect both physical and mental health.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Practice meditation.
- Exercise regularly.
- Take breaks while working.
- Sleep 7–9 hours daily.
- Talk to supportive people.

------------------------------------------------------------

ANXIETY

Anxiety involves excessive worry or nervousness. Mild anxiety may improve with relaxation techniques, while persistent anxiety may require professional support.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Practice slow breathing.
- Avoid too much caffeine.
- Exercise regularly.
- Get enough sleep.
- Seek professional help if anxiety becomes severe.

------------------------------------------------------------

LONELINESS

Feeling lonely is common and does not mean you are alone forever.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Call a friend.
- Spend time with family.
- Join clubs or communities.
- Practice hobbies.
- Go outside for a walk.

------------------------------------------------------------

HEADACHE

Headaches may occur because of dehydration, stress, lack of sleep, eye strain, or illness.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Drink water.
- Rest in a quiet room.
- Reduce screen time.
- Eat regular meals.
- Consult a doctor if headaches become severe or frequent.

------------------------------------------------------------

FEVER

Fever is an increase in body temperature, usually due to infection.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Drink plenty of fluids.
- Get adequate rest.
- Monitor your temperature.
- Take medicines only as advised.
- Seek medical attention if fever is high, persistent, or accompanied by severe symptoms.

------------------------------------------------------------

STOMACH ACHE

Stomach pain may occur because of indigestion, infection, food poisoning, constipation, or other medical conditions.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Drink water slowly.
- Eat light foods.
- Avoid spicy foods until you feel better.
- Rest.
- Seek medical care if pain is severe, persistent, or accompanied by vomiting, blood, or fever.

------------------------------------------------------------

COLD AND COUGH

The common cold is caused by viruses and usually improves within several days.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Drink warm fluids.
- Rest well.
- Gargle with warm salt water for a sore throat.
- Eat nutritious foods.
- Visit a doctor if symptoms become severe or last a long time.

------------------------------------------------------------

BODY PAIN

Body pain may occur due to exercise, illness, stress, or muscle strain.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Rest.
- Stay hydrated.
- Stretch gently.
- Use a warm compress if appropriate.
- Consult a doctor if pain is severe or persistent.

------------------------------------------------------------

BACK PAIN

Back pain can result from poor posture, muscle strain, or prolonged sitting.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Maintain good posture.
- Stretch regularly.
- Avoid lifting heavy objects incorrectly.
- Sleep on a supportive mattress.
- Seek medical advice if pain persists.

------------------------------------------------------------

PERIODS

Menstruation is a normal biological process experienced by many women every month.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Stay hydrated.
- Eat iron-rich foods.
- Get enough rest.
- Maintain personal hygiene.
- Light exercise may help improve comfort.

------------------------------------------------------------

MENSTRUAL CRAMPS

Menstrual cramps are caused by contractions of the uterus during menstruation. Mild cramps are common, but severe pain should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Use a heating pad.
- Drink warm water.
- Gentle stretching.
- Rest.
- Seek medical advice if cramps are severe or interfere with daily activities.

------------------------------------------------------------

PMS (PREMENSTRUAL SYNDROME)

PMS can occur before menstruation and may cause mood changes, bloating, fatigue, food cravings, and irritability.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Eat healthy meals.
- Exercise lightly.
- Sleep well.
- Reduce stress.
- Practice relaxation techniques.

------------------------------------------------------------

MOOD SWINGS

Mood swings involve sudden changes in emotions. They may be related to hormones, stress, lack of sleep, or emotional events.

Helpful Suggestions:
- Maintain regular sleep.
- Exercise regularly.
- Practice mindfulness.
- Listen to calming music.
- Talk to someone you trust.

------------------------------------------------------------

HEALTHY SLEEP

Adults generally benefit from 7 to 9 hours of sleep each night. Good sleep supports emotional and physical health.

Tips:
- Sleep at the same time every day.
- Avoid screens before bedtime.
- Keep your bedroom quiet and comfortable.
- Avoid caffeine late in the day.

------------------------------------------------------------

HEALTHY DIET

Eating a balanced diet supports physical and mental health.

Recommendations:
- Eat fruits and vegetables.
- Include protein in meals.
- Drink enough water.
- Reduce junk food.
- Limit sugary drinks.

------------------------------------------------------------

HYDRATION

Water is essential for normal body function.

Recommendations:
- Drink enough water throughout the day.
- Increase fluid intake during hot weather or exercise.
- Choose water instead of sugary drinks when possible.

------------------------------------------------------------

EXERCISE

Regular physical activity benefits both body and mind.

Recommendations:
- Walk for at least 30 minutes most days.
- Stretch regularly.
- Practice yoga or meditation.
- Stay active with enjoyable activities.

------------------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO SEEK MEDICAL HELP

Seek prompt medical care if you experience:
- Chest pain.
- Difficulty breathing.
- High fever that does not improve.
- Severe abdominal pain.
- Heavy or uncontrolled bleeding.
- Loss of consciousness.
- Persistent vomiting.
- Severe headache with confusion or weakness.
- Thoughts of harming yourself or others.
